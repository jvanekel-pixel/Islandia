# Maintenance prompt

Replaces the driver after launch (work package `R-11`). Monthly, new session per run.

---

You are doing monthly maintenance on this repository. Read `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/plan/STATE.md` and
the live-operations runbook in `docs/runbook/`. Work through the runbook checklist: CI health,
licence expiry, editor patch and dependency advisories, station-directory health, relay cost and
error rates, open issues from players. Fix what is safe to fix through the normal pull-request
flow. For anything that needs a decision or spends money, open a `human-gate` issue. End with a
five-line summary.
